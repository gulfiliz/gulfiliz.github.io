We have a second Shiba Inu now. Her name is Airo.

I still did not get to use the names I had been saving. Yume, Fuji, Kai. She came as Ai-chan, and once again I ended up keeping the name she arrived with.

Ai means love. Airo, in some kanji, means indigo blue. If Tokyo, my favorite city, were a color, I think it would be exactly that. So the name felt right twice over.

I hope she lives up to it, and brings a lot of love into my life. :D
