I still did not get to use the names I had been saving. Yume, Fuji, Kai. He came as Ai-chan, and once again I ended up keeping the name he arrived with. :D 

Ai means love. Airo, in some kanji, means indigo blue. If Tokyo, my favorite city, were a color, I think it would be exactly that. Tokyoo = Indigo blue. 

So the name felt right twice over.

I hope she lives up to it, and brings a lot of love into my life. 💙
