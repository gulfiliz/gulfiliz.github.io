**1) Q:** In [Gogol's Overcoat]({% post_url 2025-07-09-gogol-the-overcoat %}) you say you can't get through November without thinking about Akaki, and that when people call the story merely funny, something in you closes. That is almost exactly the reaction you describe in [My Biggest Red Flag]({% post_url 2025-01-19-intolerance-for-superficiality %}), the withdrawal when someone stays on the surface. Is Gogol a test you quietly give people?

**A:** Not on purpose, but yes. I don't hand it to people and watch. It's just that if you laughed all the way through and felt nothing at "I am your brother," I learn something I didn't ask to learn. Akaki isn't a quiz. He's a tuning fork. People ring or they don't.

**2) Q:** In [The Crisis in Fundamental Physics]({% post_url 2025-07-13-crisis-in-physics %}) you describe a field that has waited forty years for the anomaly that will crack it open, and you're entering it anyway. Is that courage, stubbornness, or faith? What do you tell yourself on the days the desert looks endless?

**A:** Stubbornness wearing faith’s coat, probably. On the days when the desert looks endless, I don’t tell myself a breakthrough is coming. I have no idea if it is. I just tell myself the desert still needs to be mapped, and I’d rather be lost in the right wilderness than comfortable in the wrong one. Most days, that is enough to open the laptop.

**5) Q:** What did you feel the first time you encountered quantum physics?

**A:** This is such a beautiful question. Thank you for asking it.
I'm usually very cautious about scientific hype, so I didn't come to quantum physics with immediate enthusiasm. I started almost defensively, as if I'd promised myself not to be seduced too easily. I remember thinking, Filiz, what you're about to learn might betray a lot of the things you thought you understood. And in a way, it did. I think I felt both betrayed and invited. :)
It felt aesthetically beautiful to me from the beginning. The mathematics was different, almost strange, but deeply elegant. Looking back, I think the part that stayed with me most is this: quantum physics tells you that the world is not obliged to satisfy human intuition!
I still find that incredibly beautiful. Cheers!
